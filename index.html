Chef Wajan is an Indonesian animated food channel: Chef Wajan, a perantau chef, lives another food culture for seven days or answers one food "kenapa?", with every fact sourced.

Chef Wajan Studio Tools is a small, private desktop tool that the channel owner runs on his own computer to manage his own channel through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API:

  • upload the channel's own finished videos, Shorts, captions and thumbnails, and schedule them;
  • update titles, descriptions and playlists of the channel's own videos;
  • read the channel's own analytics (views, watch time, audience retention) to plan the next videos.

It is not a public service. It has no user accounts, it is used only by the owner of the Chef Wajan channel, and it never accesses anyone else's channel or data.

Bahasa Indonesia: Chef Wajan Studio Tools adalah alat pribadi milik pemilik channel Chef Wajan untuk mengunggah, menjadwalkan, dan membaca analitik video di channel-nya sendiri melalui YouTube API. Bukan layanan publik dan tidak mengakses data orang lain.
